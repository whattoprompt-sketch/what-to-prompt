# The Sovereign Prompt Protocol: Theoretical Framework 🏛

WhatToPrompt is built on the **Sovereign Prompt Protocol**, a philosophy of prompt engineering that moves beyond simple refinement into **Structural Transformation**.

## 🔴 The Problem: AI Parroting
Standard prompt rewriters often "parrot" a user's input. If a user says "I want to write a story about a cat," the rewriter might say "Generate a creative story about a feline." This retains the limited scope and novice tone of the original intent.

## 🟢 Our Solution: The Transformation Delta
The Sovereign Protocol introduces a **Delta** between user intent and AI instruction.

### 1. Context Isolation (The "Black Box" Payload)
Instead of feeding your raw draft to the final LLM, the Alchemy Engine:
- **Scrubs** all novice keywords.
- **Extracts** the core intent (e.g., "Cat Story" -> "Creative Fiction").
- **Maps** that intent to a high-fidelity "Architect Payload."

### 2. Credential Stacking
We don't just assign a role; we **stack credentials**. 
- **User Input**: "Teacher"
- **Sovereign Profile**: "Senior Pedagogy Strategist and Learning Experience Designer with a focus on cognitive load theory."
By providing deep history and credentials, we unlock the model's "expert" neurons.

### 3. Structural Axioms (Model-Specific Optimization)
Different LLMs "think" differently based on their architecture and RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) history.
- **Anthropic Axiom**: Claude prefers XML tags for data separation.
- **OpenAI Axiom**: GPT prefers Markdown headers and explicit "System" instructions.
- **Llama Axiom**: Llama-3 thrives on structured instruction blocks (`[INST]`).

The Sovereign Protocol applies these axioms automatically at the generation layer.

---

## 📈 Optimization Frameworks

The protocol mandates the injection of advanced reasoning frameworks when specific thresholds are met:

### **Chain-of-Thought (CoT)**
Used for technical and logical tasks. By injecting "Let’s think step by step," we force the model to allocate more compute to its internal reasoning path.

### **Tree-of-Thought (ToT)**
Used for strategic and highly creative tasks. The prompt forces the model to simulate multiple independent expert personae and "vote" on the best strategic path.

### **AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)**
Hard-injected for all marketing and sales prompts to ensure conversion-oriented output.

---

## ⚖️ The Quality Audit: The Objective Lens
Every prompt must pass through an automated audit gate. This uses an independent LLM instance to score the prompt based on:
1. **Separation of Concerns**: Is the Role clearly distinct from the Task?
2. **Negative Constraints**: Are "Forbidden Tones" or "Output Limitations" explicitly stated?
3. **Variable Specificity**: Are placeholders used to allow for dynamic data injection?
